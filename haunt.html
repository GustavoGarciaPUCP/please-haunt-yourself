      About HAUNT OS

      HAUNT OS 4 · build v9 ·

      A fictional Halloween desktop made for Kiroween 2026. One unfinished job from 31 October 1987, four programs, one letter.

      What is real here

      The programs, the windows and everything you make in them. Mara, her 1987 session, the glass and the mail are fiction. Nothing leaves this browser: there is no real mail, network, camera or access to your computer’s programs.

      Keyboard

      • Tab moves between controls. Escape closes menus and panels; it never closes a window.
      • A window’s icon opens its menu: Move and Size use the arrow keys (Shift for larger steps), Enter to finish.
      • ECHO’s wipe cursor: arrow keys move it; hold Space to wipe. A photo frame: arrow keys move it, + and − resize it.
      • CANVAS: Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo the letter. In “The photograph keeps”, the arrow keys choose an option.
      • A panel that does not fit its window scrolls; Tab reaches it, and the arrow keys scroll it.
