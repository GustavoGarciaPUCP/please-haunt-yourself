# TERMINAL v2 — short neutral playtest protocol

This is a protocol only. **No sessions have been run, and no results exist.** It is meant for at least three people who do not program. The hypotheses below are targets to compare against, not findings.

## Setup (per person, about 10 minutes)

- Use a fresh browser profile, or clear site data, so the first-run guide appears and no saved ending exists.
- Run it on the device the person normally uses: phone or laptop. Record which.
- Say only: *"This is a short Halloween story inside a pretend computer. Please think aloud. I can't help, but you can stop at any time."*
- Do not explain the bell, the note or the buttons. Do not use the words *callback, process, loop, output.*
- Observe without prompting. If the person is stuck for 2 minutes, note it, then say only *"What have you tried so far?"*

## Observe (write down what happens, not interpretations)

| # | Observation | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O1 | Time from opening TERMINAL to the first press of Watch it try to leave | seconds |
| O2 | First object selected (Note / Ghost / Bell / Way out), and when | name, seconds |
| O3 | Number of farewells (Guide or Force) tried while the bell could still ring | count |
| O4 | Hint used? Observation hint only, or also "Tell me exactly"? | none / 1 / 2 |
| O5 | Ending reached: kept note, blank page, shadow only, or none | ending |
| O6 | Pressed Take back the prompt? Typed something after Say something? | yes / no |
| O7 | Visible confusion or hesitation, as close to verbatim as possible | quotes |

## Ask afterwards (open questions, in this order)

1. "What kept bringing the ghost back?"
2. "What did you change, and what did that change do?"
3. "What was kept at the end, and was that your choice?"
4. "How did you know you were finished?"
5. "Was anything confusing or annoying?"

## Hypotheses (targets, stated before any session)

- **H1.** At least 2 of 3 name the bell as the cause (question 1) without having used "Tell me exactly".
- **H2.** At least 2 of 3 correctly say that hushing did not free the ghost, or that the note choice decided what stayed (question 2 or 3).
- **H3.** All 3 can say what was kept (the note, or nothing).
- **H4.** All 3 know they were finished, and point to Take back the prompt, the "At peace" banner or their own words echoed back.
- **H5.** No one needs the Under the hood section.
