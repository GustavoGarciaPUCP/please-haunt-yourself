# First-visit protocol (v7)

An observation script for one person who has not seen the project and does not program. It updates `../os-case-v4/first-visit-protocol.md` for the current build and adds the three questions review 28 and prompt07 name. **It has not been run: nobody was available during the November 9 Kiro session.** Automated checks are not a substitute. An AI walkthrough, if one is ever run, must be reported as a simulation.

## Setup (5 minutes)

1. `npm.cmd run build`, then `npm.cmd run preview -- --port 5174 --strictPort`.
2. Open a **private window** at `http://127.0.0.1:5174/haunt.html` at 1366×768 or larger (a fresh desk: the boot line, the callout and an open job). HAUNT → About HAUNT OS must say *build v7* (on the current build, *build v9*: free-text-v8 changed only POSTMAN's layout of typed text, and fluid-typing-v9 only the work a key in POSTMAN causes and the active-window frame, so this protocol applies unchanged).
3. Sound stays off unless the participant turns it on.

## Say only this

"This is a fictional computer. Something on it is unfinished. Do what you think makes sense. Please think aloud. I won't help unless you ask, and you can stop at any time."

## Observe (do not prompt)

Note the time at each moment and the words the person uses.

| Moment | What to note |
| --- | --- |
| First action | Did they find Look inside or type? How long did it take? |
| The bell | Did they try Muffle the bell first? Did they say why the minute kept coming back? |
| The glass | Did they notice the hand at TERMINAL's edge? Did they move the window? |
| ECHO | Did they choose Desktop glass? Did they read the chips, or *Unfinished note* on the session window? |
| CANVAS | Did they point at or choose an option in "The photograph keeps"? Did they look at the large letter while choosing? |
| POSTMAN | Who did they send it to first, and why? If a question appeared, did they read it, and what did they answer? |
| The end | Did they read "You were the next person"? Did they notice the glass clearing, or only the letter? |
| Help | Which hints did they open, and at which phase? |

## Ask afterwards

1. "What happened, in your own words?"
2. "Why did the minute keep starting again?" (the cause of the return: the call, not the bell)
3. "What does your photograph show? Did you choose that?" (whether they recognize their photograph choice)
4. "Who was the letter for?" (whether they connect the recipient to the person at the desk)
5. "Was there a moment you felt stuck or unsure?"

## Record honestly

Record the total time and the time per phase, every place where they got stuck, every help request, any wrong recipient and how they recovered, and their answers to questions 2–4 in their own words. If nobody tests it, say so. Do not report agent walkthroughs as people.
