## Internal screenshot review

The desktop final, opening and Spanish 390px scene were inspected. The paper stage, workshop controls and fixed gallery are visually coherent, and the full frame remains recognisable. A home aperture now closes when its frame moves, and a hand visibly grips the carried frame at the ending. The opening note was moved away from the feet so its explanation does not obscure the contact it teaches.

The mobile figure remains too small, and the ending still needs a fuller turn/pickup/carry sequence. The opening is implemented and software-checked; its ability to teach a new player has not been established.
