## Observe discovery

Have someone who has not read the solution try the game without coaching. Record what they believe the shadow, frame and destination do; where they hesitate; which failures they understand; and what they think happened at the ending. Assistance and hints must be recorded rather than hidden. AI role-play does not satisfy this step.

The next changes should address observed confusion. Do not add another puzzle merely to increase a count, or explain away the central discovery with an unsolicited solution.

- Give arrival, turning, gripping and taking the frame a readable sequence; preserve full-foot clearance on the fixed gallery before pickup.
- Review framing and object size on narrow screens; fitting the page alone is insufficient.
- Check keyboard focus, pointer cancellation, motion choices, silent play and both languages through the whole journey.

Assign bounded implementation or review tasks to Kiro, using the native authenticated CLI. There is no need for the participant to copy prompts into the IDE while this route works. If an IDE-specific review later provides a concrete benefit, prepare the exact task and explain its purpose.
