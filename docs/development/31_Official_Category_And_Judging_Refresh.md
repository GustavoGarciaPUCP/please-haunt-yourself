## Category recommendation: Costume Contest

There are currently four category choices in the submission form: Resurrection, Frankenstein, Skeleton Crew and Costume Contest. The recommendation is **Costume Contest**. This check reads the available choices; it does not verify or alter a saved submission's selected category.

Its official description is:

> Build any app but show us a haunting user interface that’s polished and unforgettable. Bring in spooky design elements that enhance your app’s function.

Source: [official rules, section 4](https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules).

**Assessment of our fit:** the Halloween imagery participates in the interaction. TERMINAL's returning apparition supplies the material observed by ECHO; the kept capture becomes the source for CANVAS's letter; POSTMAN's actual delivery supplies the receipt that TERMINAL accepts. The player's work and the supernatural scene affect the same case. This is the clearest category argument in the current design.

The desktop aesthetic alone does not establish resurrection of an obsolete technology. Distinct fictional programs alone do not establish the incompatible-technology premise of Frankenstein. A reusable template and two separate applications are not this project's intended deliverable. Switching category to match those alternatives is not justified by v8.

The category does not prescribe a mobile app. A desktop-browser target is consistent with the intended-platform requirements. PC polish remains the product priority; do not turn this rules refresh into a mobile redesign.

An entertainment project can make its audience/value case through the experience it actually provides. Do not invent an educational, therapeutic or productivity benefit to satisfy the impact question. The immediate product risk is a player following instructions to prepare mail without understanding or caring about the mystery. Observe that risk before adding mechanics.
